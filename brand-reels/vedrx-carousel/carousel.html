A little clarity goes a long way

Your questions. Thoughtful answers.

Q.How do I start planning my treatment in India?

Send a medical tourism enquiry with your treatment needs, country, and contact details. Our care team will discuss your requirements and guide the next steps, including the medical reports needed for hospital review.

Q.Can I compare hospitals before deciding?

Yes. Explore hospitals by city and treatment, shortlist your options, and compare their listed specialties. Ask our care team to confirm the specific branch, availability, credentials, and treatment suitability before booking.

Q.What happens after I return home?

Discuss follow-up appointments, prescriptions, recovery instructions, and contact arrangements with your treating hospital before travelling home. Our care team can help coordinate further communication.

Q.Do you guarantee treatment outcomes?

No. Medical suitability and outcomes depend on individual circumstances and must be discussed with the treating clinical team. VedRxTours supports coordination and does not replace medical advice.
