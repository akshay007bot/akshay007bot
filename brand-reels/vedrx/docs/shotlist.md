# Ved Rx Tours: brand reel shot list
30 s · 80 bpm (1 bar = 3.0 s = one hook) · 40 beats · format: 9x16 (Instagram Reel)
Music: synthesized warm tabla bed, D major · VO: none (on-screen text carries the message)

Client brief (WhatsApp): a new hook every ~3 s so people reach the CTA; B-roll must match the text.
Rule used here: **every line of text is shown over the exact thing it names.** All words are the
brand's own (vedrxtours.in copy); no invented claims, prices or results.

Look: cinematic. Deep navy (#0f1729) frame, photos graded slightly warm with soft vignette and film
grain, slow log-space dolly on every photo. Headlines in Playfair Display (site's serif), small labels
in Inter caps. One accent: Ved Rx teal. Text lands word-by-word on the beat.
