02 · WHY ANNOVA 360
Five Commitments.
Zero Surprises.

ANNOVA 360 is the design-build studio for clients who've seen precision get lost between the drawing and the site, and won't risk it again.

01
What You Approve Is What You Get

Precision execution. Every material, finish, dimension and detail is locked at design sign-off and delivered exactly as specced. No dilution, no on-site improvisation, no surprises at handover.

02
One Design Partner, Zero Handoffs

The same team that designs your space executes it, so there's no gap between design intent and site interpretation.

03
Client-Embedded Process

You're not a stakeholder who signs off at milestones · you're inside the process, with structured weekly walkthroughs and real-time decisions made with you, not around you.

04
Spec-Level Documentation

Every drawing, BOQ and material call-out is detailed to the tolerance of a build document, not a mood board.

05
Fixed Scope, Fixed Timeline, Fixed Budget

Precision isn't just aesthetic, it's operational. We commit to the scope, cost and schedule agreed at the start, and we deliver against it.
