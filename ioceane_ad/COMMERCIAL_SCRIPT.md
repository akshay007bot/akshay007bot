# iOceane: "Seen. Remembered." Live-action commercial (30 s)

**Idea:** A Bengaluru café founder whose brand nobody notices. The iOceane team takes the brief, then shoots, designs and builds. The same café ends up full. This is the agency's promise shown as a story, without explaining it: *we make brands infinite.*

**Look:** a cinematic commercial. Anamorphic 2.39 framing inside 16:9, shallow depth of field and gentle camera moves. The grade goes from cold teal and blue at night to warm golden daylight. The iOceane blue (#009FFF) and mint (#00FFAB) appear as practical lights in the studio scenes.

**Sound:** an ambient opening with rain on the window. A heartbeat pulse comes in when the team appears, a beat drop on the montage, and a big hit on the logo.
