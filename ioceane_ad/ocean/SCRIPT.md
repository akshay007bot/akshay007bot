# iOceane: "An Ocean of Ideas" (30 s)

**Concept:** the brand name is the idea. A single drop of light, one idea, falls into a dark ocean. Its ripples grow into a wave, and the wave rises into everything iOceane builds. Each service appears as a 3D structure made from the same ocean particles. The ocean then fills with ideas, and the particles come together to form the iOceane logo.

**Look:** the brand's own visual system, taken from ioceane.com. Deep navy background, glowing ocean blue #009FFF and mint #00FFAB, futuristic 3D particles with bloom, and Comfortaa/Inter typography. No stock people.

**Sound:** an original synthesized score. A deep-ocean drone, a water-drop plink with a sub boom, ripple chimes, a whoosh on the wave, a driving pulse under the shapes with a shimmer on each morph, an airy major-key bed for the ocean of ideas, and a resolving hit on the logo.

**Build:** `node render3.js land|port` renders `ocean.html` (three.js, deterministic per frame). Run `python3 make_score.py build/score.wav` for the score. The VO is `vo_ocean.mp3` (ElevenLabs, Prashant Nair).
