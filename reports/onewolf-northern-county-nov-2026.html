The month's content reached mostly new people, and the pricing message did most of the work. The two gaps that matter most are short video watch time and missing conversion tracking on the website.

Key takeaways

  • WinThe ₹2,999 per sq ft pricing creative (27 Oct) was the top post with 513 views, 3.6× the next best. A clear price is the strongest hook for this audience.
  • WinFacebook watch time rose 219% and 3-second video views rose 120%, so video is starting to get attention.
  • WatchFacebook views fell 97% against the previous period. Daily views dropped to near zero on 30 Oct – 4 Nov and 16–17 Nov even on days we posted, so reach appears to follow paid promotion rather than posting.
  • WatchNo Facebook viewer watched a full minute of video (0 one-minute views). The first 3 seconds and overall length need work.
  • Fix nowThe project page has no Meta Pixel, so leads and site visits from Facebook and Instagram can't be tracked or retargeted.

What the data says

  • PatternThree active windows: 28–29 Oct, 5–15 Nov and 18–24 Nov. These look like boost or ad flights. Please confirm against Ads Manager spend.
  • PatternThe busiest posting stretch (30 Oct – 3 Nov) earned almost no views, so organic Facebook reach for the page is close to zero.
  • VideoWatch time of 2,362 seconds across 97 three-second viewers is about 24 seconds each. Viewers who stay do watch, but very few stop scrolling.

Why views fell 97%

A 97% fall means the previous period had roughly 500K views. That level only comes from a large paid campaign, so the drop most likely reflects lower ad spend this month rather than weaker content. Engagement quality improved over the same period: 3-second views and watch time both more than doubled.

Recommendation: report paid and organic views separately from next month so the trend is readable.

Content insights

  • PricePrice-led creatives win on reach: “₹2,999 per sq ft, inclusive of land + farm house” got 513 views. The early-bird and free-villa offer (21 Nov) was the second-most engaging post.
  • EducationExplainers like “Why land beats another apartment” and “Thinking about it? Let's answer what's on your mind” appear in both top-5 lists. Buyers want reasons as well as visuals.
  • Lifestyle“Step into a life where every day feels like a retreat” (5 Nov) got the most interactions (5). Aspirational lifestyle copy gets saves and shares.
  • GapInteractions per creative are low (3–5 on the best posts). Every post needs one clear ask, such as “Comment SITE VISIT” or “Save this for later”.

05

Plan for December

Focus on turning reach into followers, leads and tracked site visits.

Content

  • 2 price-led creatives a week: price per sq ft, total ticket size and EMI examples.
  • Weekly Reel under 20 seconds that opens on the land in the first 2 seconds, with on-screen price.
  • FAQ carousels from real enquiries: water, power, legal papers, resale.
  • Story polls and question stickers for the warm follower base.

Paid & tracking

  • Install the Meta Pixel and CAPI before the next ad flight.
  • Run an always-on lead campaign so reach doesn't drop to zero between boosts.
  • Retarget video viewers and profile visitors with the early-bird offer.
  • Report paid and organic views separately.

Website & SEO

  • Ship the H1, image and RERA fixes from section 04.
  • Add a Google Business Profile post and site-visit photos every week.
  • Publish 2 blog guides targeting “farm house near Nandi Hills” and “farm house for sale Devanahalli”.
  • Submit the sitemap in Search Console and track impressions.
